# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Merchant fraud, risk, and incident-response operators reviewing high-stakes account-compromise cases in bright offices, dim operations rooms, and long-running desktop sessions. Desktop is the primary full investigation and decision surface; tablet supports meaningful review and approval; mobile supports status and focused review rather than full investigative parity.

Operators need to reconstruct what happened, distinguish malicious, legitimate, and genuinely uncertain activity, understand authoritative financial exposure, inspect policy and approval decisions, follow action execution through reconciliation and verification, identify unresolved work, and trace every conclusion to immutable evidence and audit provenance.

## Product Purpose

RECLAIM is an incident command workspace for merchant-controlled fraud containment. The case is the central object, and every surface is a perspective on the same evolving incident: timeline, evidence, attribution, exposure, policy, approval, action, reconciliation, verification, escalation, and audit.

Success means an authorized operator can quickly answer what happened, what remains at risk, what RECLAIM proposed, what deterministic policy decided, what was actually executed and verified, and what the human must do next—without editing authoritative records or confusing advisory analysis with execution authority. LIVE and REPLAY must remain unmistakably truthful.

## Brand Personality

Distinctive, precise, trustworthy. RECLAIM should carry a recognizable premium identity over the discipline of an institutional risk-operations console. It should feel deliberate, technical-authoritative, operational, and calm under pressure rather than theatrical or intimidating.

## Anti-references

- Generic SOC dashboards, cybersecurity consoles, fintech admin panels, executive KPI dashboards, trading platforms, gambling interfaces, and crypto interfaces.
- Generic AI SaaS styling, cream or beige SaaS palettes, purple AI gradients, glassmorphism, neon hacker green, generic dark security themes, fintech navy, and navy-plus-gold.
- Disconnected card collections, nested cards, giant decorative metrics, fake charts, graph-first navigation, badge spam, or visual density without operational value.
- Manipulative confirmations, approval modals as the default workflow, ambiguous disabled actions in REPLAY, or any presentation that makes uncertain evidence look like weakly malicious evidence.

## Design Principles

1. **The case is the workspace.** Keep investigation, decision context, containment state, and provenance connected as views of one incident rather than separate dashboards.
2. **Understanding precedes action.** Make the reconstructed timeline and supporting evidence the primary investigative spine; keep policy, approval, and containment decisions persistent but secondary.
3. **Authority must be visible.** Distinguish advisory analysis, deterministic policy, independent approval, Action Gateway execution, reconciliation, verification, escalation, and terminal state without implying that one substitutes for another.
4. **Uncertainty is a first-class outcome.** Present unresolved attribution, UNKNOWN execution, and inconclusive verification as distinct operational states that require attention, never as softened failure or probable maliciousness.
5. **Operational truth over spectacle.** Emphasize remaining exposure, next human decision, immutable provenance, and truthful LIVE/REPLAY mode using controlled hierarchy and restrained state-explaining motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA in the approved dark operator theme. Support complete keyboard navigation, strong visible focus, meaningful screen-reader structure and announcements, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, and status communication that never depends on color alone. Error, warning, success, attribution, mode, and lifecycle states must combine text, iconography, structure, and accessible names. Important state changes must remain understandable without animation. Any future light theme must be designed and validated independently rather than produced by inversion.
